Mary lives in a small town with her family. She has a mother, a father, and one brother. Her brother is ten years old. Every morning, Mary gets up at seven. She washes her face, eats breakfast, and goes to school. She usually walks because the school is near her home.

At school, Mary studies English, math, history, and art. Her favorite subject is English. She likes reading short stories and learning new words. At lunch, she eats with her friends. They often talk about school, music, and animals.

After school, Mary goes home and does her homework. Then she helps her mother in the kitchen. In the evening, the family has dinner together. Sometimes they watch a film, and sometimes Mary reads her book.

On Saturday, Mary visits her grandmother. Her grandmother has a small garden with flowers and two cats. Mary plays with the cats and helps in the garden. On Sunday, she rests at home and prepares her school bag for Monday.
